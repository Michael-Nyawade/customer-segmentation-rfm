Methodology

Business Problem

Not all customers are equally valuable, and treating them the same wastes marketing budget and misses opportunities. This tool identifies which accounts are high-value, loyal, at risk of churning, or worth investing in for growth.

How It Works

Purchase history is summarized into three measures per account: Recency (days since last purchase), Frequency (number of orders), and Monetary (total spend). These feed a rule-based scoring system and a K-Means clustering model, labeled by ranking each cluster's average spend rather than a fixed index - so labeling stays correct however the model is retrained.

Dataset

UCI Online Retail Dataset - around 540,000 transactions from a UK-based online retailer, 2010-2011.
